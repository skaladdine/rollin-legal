# Credits

Rollin is built with the help of these data sources and services.

## Gym data

- Gym listings include data from **OpenStreetMap**, © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License ([openstreetmap.org/copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)).
- Some gym listings come from the public club directory of the **CFJJB** (Confédération Française de Jiu-Jitsu Brésilien) and from the **sport.brussels** venue directory.

## Maps and places

- Maps, city search and place locations are provided by **Google** (Google Maps Platform). Use of Google Maps is subject to the Google Maps Terms of Service ([maps.google.com/help/terms_maps](https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/)) and Google's Privacy Policy ([policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).

## Software

- Rollin is made with Flutter and Supabase. The open-source licences of the software used in the app are listed in the app, under Profile → Terms & Privacy → Open-source licences.
