# Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-24. Last updated 24 November 2026.

This policy explains what personal data Rollin uses, why, who sees it, and your rights. The data controller is **Armoneo**, a SASU (simplified joint-stock company with a single shareholder) registered in France, [REGISTERED OFFICE ADDRESS] ("we"). Contact: [jjb.rollin@gmail.com](mailto:jjb.rollin@gmail.com).

## 1. What we collect

**Your account**

- Your email address and password. The password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form by our hosting provider; we can't read it.

**Your profile** (shown to other signed-in members)

- First name, profile photo, belt, weight category (for example "Medium-heavy", worked out from your weight and, if you give it, your gender), city, training goal, bio.
- Your gender, if you choose to give it (optional). You can hide it from your profile, and women can choose to be visible only to other women: then only women find them in search and on the map and can contact them, apart from people they already chat with or are training partners with.
- Training preferences: styles (Gi, No-Gi, Grappling) and preferred intensity.
- Your availability: the days, times and dates you choose to share.

**Your private details** (shown only to your training partners)

- Last name, exact weight, gym and training area. Your partners also see your exact age, worked out from your birthdate; they never see the birthdate itself.

**Your birthdate** (private)

- Used only to check that you are 18 or older, to work out your age division (for example "Master 1") and to show your exact age to your training partners. Other members see the division, never your birthdate or exact age.

**Your location**

- The city you choose from suggestions and the training area you type. We don't use your device's GPS.
- For most members, your map pin and the distance to you are based on your city. Only your training partners see your pin at your training area, for which we look up the area's position. Your pin is always moved by a fixed random distance of up to about 1.5 km, and distances shown to others are rounded (for example "under 5 km"). Your exact position is never stored or shown.

**What you do in the app**

- Messages, chat requests and the sessions you propose, accept, decline or cancel (time, duration, place).
- Ratings: the stars you give after a roll and the optional private note. Other members only see an average and a count, never who rated them. Only we can read the notes.
- Your training partners: who you proposed to or accepted, who proposed to you, and when a partnership started or ended. Declined requests are kept so the other person isn't asked again too soon; the person who sent one isn't told it was declined.
- Partner codes: the codes you create, when they expire, and who used yours and when. We also note when someone types a wrong code (who and when, not what was typed), to limit guessing; these notes are deleted after one day.
- The people you block, and the reports you send (reason, optional details, and the chat concerned).
- Notifications about your sessions, chats, ratings and partners, your notification settings, and the date you accepted these terms.
- If you allow push notifications on your phone: a technical push address for the device (a token from Apple or Google), whether it's an iPhone or Android, and the app's language, so the notification is in your language. It's removed when you log out of that phone.
- Short technical logs of your searches, used only to limit abuse: when you searched for grapplers (deleted after one hour) and when you searched for a city (deleted after 24 hours). They don't contain what you typed.

**When you contact support**

- When you send a request from Profile → Support, we receive your message, the reply address you give, and, added by the app so we can help, the app version, device model, operating system version and your Rollin user ID (the form shows what is sent). We keep the request until your account is deleted, and it is emailed to us through Resend so we can reply.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we don't sell your data.

## 2. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

- **To provide Rollin** (your account, profile, search, map, chats, sessions, ratings): this is necessary to perform our agreement with you (GDPR article 6(1)(b)).
- **To keep members safe** (age check, blocking, reports, moderation, rate limits, review notes): our legitimate interest in a safe service and our duty to enforce our terms (article 6(1)(f)), and, for the age check, the agreement itself, since Rollin is for adults only.
- **To meet legal obligations**, for example answering a lawful request from the authorities (article 6(1)(c)).

## 3. Who can see your data

- **Other signed-in members** see your profile, age division, availability, rating average and approximate location (based on your city), but not your email, birthdate, last name, exact weight or age, gym or training area (nor your gender if you hide it). People you have blocked, and people who blocked you, don't see each other; a woman who chose to be visible only to women isn't shown to others, apart from people she already chats with or is partners with (including anyone she gives her partner code to).
- **Your training partners** also see your private details (last name, exact weight and age, gym, training area) and your pin at your training area. Someone becomes your partner only when you both agree: by accepting a request, or by using a partner code that one of you shared. Either of you can end it at any time, and blocking ends it. They then stop seeing your private details.
- **Someone who has your partner code** sees your public profile (first name, photo, belt, weight category, city) when they type it, even if they couldn't find you in search otherwise, and can become your partner with it. Share a code only with people you know; you can cancel it at any time, and it stops working after 3 days or once it's used.
- **The people you talk to** see your messages and the sessions you arrange with them.
- **We** can access data to run the service and handle reports.
- **Service providers** who process data for us, under contract:
  - **Supabase** (database, accounts, photo storage, server functions), servers in the European Union (Stockholm, Sweden).
  - **Google** (Places and Geocoding to suggest cities and place training areas; Google Maps to show the map). When you search for a city, what you type is sent to Google. When a map is shown, your device loads it from Google, which receives your IP address.
  - **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** and, on iPhones, **Apple Push Notification service**, to deliver push notifications. They receive your device's push token and the notification's text (for example "New message from Alex").
  - **Resend** (email): when a member is reported, we receive an alert email containing only the reason, a reference number and the time, without names or message content. Support requests are emailed to us in full, so that we can reply.

Some of these providers are based in the United States or may process data there. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Gym listings come from public sources such as OpenStreetMap; using them shares no data about you.

## 4. How long we keep it

- Your data is kept while your account exists.
- Search logs are deleted after one hour (grappler searches) or 24 hours (city searches). Notes of wrong partner codes are deleted after one day.
- Earlier profile photos may stay in storage until you delete your account.
- **When you delete your account** (Profile → Delete account), it happens immediately: we erase your profile, photos, birthdate, styles, availability, notifications, blocks, sessions, partnerships, partner codes, device tokens, the ratings you received, your support requests and your terms acceptance.
- **What stays, without your name:** messages you sent remain in the other person's chat, shown as from a "Deleted user"; ratings you gave still count in the other person's average; reports stay so we can deal with abuse.

## 5. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or erase your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy in a portable format. You can correct most of it yourself in Edit Profile and delete everything with Delete account. For anything else, email [jjb.rollin@gmail.com](mailto:jjb.rollin@gmail.com); we answer within one month.

You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the **CNIL** ([www.cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).

## 6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access is limited by strict database rules so that members only reach what they are allowed to see.

## 7. Children

Rollin is only for people aged 18 and over. We refuse sign-ups with a birthdate under 18.

## 8. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the app will ask you to accept the new version.
