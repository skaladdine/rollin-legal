## 1. What Rollin is

Rollin helps Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and grappling practitioners find training partners near them, talk to each other and agree on sessions ("rolls").

Members can also become **training partners**, when both agree: partners see a few more details about each other (such as full name, gym and training area). A **partner code** lets someone you already know become your partner at once; anyone who has the code can use it while it's valid, so only share it with people you want as partners. Either partner can end a partnership at any time.

Rollin only puts people in touch. We don't organise, supervise or attend any session. We don't check anyone's identity, skill, belt, health or gym, and we don't vouch for any member or gym listed in the app.

## 3. Training safely

Grappling is a contact sport with a real risk of injury. You train with other members **at your own risk** and are responsible for your own decisions.

- Train in a gym or another appropriate place, ideally with a qualified instructor present.
- Agree on the intensity before you start, tap early, and stop if anything feels wrong.
- Only train if you are in good health; ask a doctor if in doubt.
- Meet new people in public places or at a gym, and tell someone where you are going.
